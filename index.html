Steering & voice help

Look straight to center. Turn your head to steer.

Say “Rover forward,” “Rover reverse,” “Rover left,” or “Rover right.” Say “stop” or press Esc to stop, “resume” to drive again, or “recenter” to calibrate.

Forward motion pauses below 20 cm or when the front sensor has no echo. Motion pauses when tracking or rover data is lost.

The camera view is mirrored. Use desktop Chrome or Edge over HTTPS. Close XRPCode before connecting the Red Board.
